Masthead — engraved caps

Same word, same tracking discipline, five cuts. Pick one; I'll build the rest of the type scale around it.

A Cormorant
light · 300
AYYAZ
Hairline serif, high contrast. Reads engraved on paper — closest to a printed plate. Risk: thin strokes get fragile on a dirty ground.
B Jost
extralight · 200
AYYAZ
Geometric sans, 1920s German poster. This is actually what the Der Mensch plate letters look like. Quiet, modern, no wedding smell.
C Bodoni Moda
regular · 400
AYYAZ
Didone. Brutal thick/thin contrast — loudest of the five and the most fashion-magazine. Pairs violently well with flat vermillion.
D Cinzel
regular · 400
AYYAZ
Literal Roman inscription — carved in stone. Maximum gravitas, and the most overused of the five. Included so you can rule it out on sight.
E Marcellus
regular · 400
AYYAZ
Inscriptional like Cinzel but softer and far less used. Flared stems, slightly hand-cut. My pick if you want engraved without the toga.
04

Pressure

Buttons. Hover each one. No pills, no rounded corners, no drop shadows with blur — printed objects, not glass.

A · letterpress Hard offset shadow. The block physically sinks into the page when pressed.
B · ink flood Colour rises from the baseline like ink soaking up. Quiet until touched.
C · ruled link For tertiary things. The rule thickens into a drawn stroke.
D · cut corner A clipped shape instead of a radius. Reads as a physical tag.
